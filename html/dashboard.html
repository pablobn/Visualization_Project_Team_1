Mesophilic

What it is for: The compost starts to activate and the first microorganisms break down easy organic matter.

What is needed: Fresh material, oxygen, balanced moisture, and temperatures that begin to rise into the 20–40 °C range.

Thermophilic

What it is for: Fast decomposition of complex material and strong pathogen reduction at peak biological activity.

What is needed: Enough microbial activity, a nitrogen-carbon mix that fuels heating, oxygen, and sustained temperatures above 40 °C.

Cooling

What it is for: The intense heat begins to drop while remaining material keeps decomposing more slowly.

What is needed: The thermophilic peak must have already happened, then temperature gradually falls as easily degradable matter is consumed.

Maturation

What it is for: Final stabilization, humus formation, and production of safer, more mature compost.

What is needed: Stable lower temperatures, enough time, moderate moisture, and continued aeration without re-heating the pile.

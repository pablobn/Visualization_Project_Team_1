About the project

This project connects multiple sensors distributed across the campus to measure temperature, humidity, and composting process conditions. The interface is designed to compare zones, detect fast changes, and communicate results clearly.

What you can see

The dashboard shows current Breeding Trials, Shelter, and Outdoor readings, plus a visual compost bin view split into Top, Middle, and Bottom zones.
